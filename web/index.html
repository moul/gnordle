Why the answer is on the page

A realm cannot keep a secret. Its source is public the moment it is deployed, and every derivation available to it is derivable by anybody who can read a chain. So this one does not pretend. What the chain can do is prove how you played: hard mode is enforced on chain, so a letter you have found must stay where you found it and a letter you know is in the word must appear in every later guess. Your streak is a proof.
